# Hipistock Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026  
**Publisher:** Erdal Demirci  
**Product:** Hipistock Chrome extension (any Shopify store → any Hipicon seller account)

## We do not collect user data

Erdal Demirci does **not** collect, receive, store, sell, or share any user data from the extension. There is no Hipistock backend, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

## What the extension does

Transfers your own Shopify catalog (prices and stock) to your own Hipicon seller panel. Not limited to a single brand or store. Processing runs in your browser.

## Local device storage only

Settings and a temporary catalog cache may be kept in Chrome local extension storage on your computer. That data is not sent to the publisher.

## Third-party requests you initiate

Your browser may contact Shopify, Hipicon, and public FX providers solely to complete the transfer you start. Erdal Demirci does not receive a copy.

## Deletion

Uninstall the extension in `chrome://extensions`, or clear data via in-extension controls.
