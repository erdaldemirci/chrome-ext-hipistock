Hipistock Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026
Applies to the Hipistock Chrome extension published by Erdal Demirci — a general tool for any Shopify store syncing to any Hipicon seller account.

We do not collect user data

Erdal Demirci (the publisher) does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any user data from the Extension. There is no Hipistock backend, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service.

What the Extension does

Hipistock helps you transfer your own Shopify product catalog (prices and stock) to your own Hipicon seller panel. It is not limited to a single brand or store. Processing happens in your browser only.

Data that stays on your device

To work between sessions, the Extension may keep settings and a temporary catalog cache in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer (for example Shopify store URL, Hipicon store name, FX rate, optional Shopify Admin API token if you enter one). That data is not transmitted to the publisher (Erdal Demirci).

Requests your browser may make

Solely to perform the transfer you start, your browser may contact services you already use:

  • Shopify (catalog / Admin session)
  • Hipicon (seller panel / Excel upload APIs)
  • Public FX sources (TCMB and optional fallbacks) for USD→TRY conversion

Those requests are made by your browser to those providers under their own policies. Erdal Demirci does not receive a copy.

Deletion

Uninstall the Extension in chrome://extensions to remove its local storage, or clear data with the Extension’s own controls.
